# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on advancing cardiovascular modeling, clinical education, and medical research.

---

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Respectful Communication**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
- **Constructive Feedback**: Providing and accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- **Collaboration**: Focusing on what is best for the community and science
- **Empathy**: Showing empathy and kindness toward other community members
- **Scientific Rigor**: Maintaining high standards for code quality and validation
- **Attribution**: Properly citing sources, literature, and contributions
- **Professionalism**: Demonstrating professionalism in all interactions
- **Inclusivity**: Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- **Responsibility**: Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes

### Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment**: Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- **Discrimination**: Discriminatory jokes, language, or behavior
- **Inappropriate Content**: Sexual language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
- **Privacy Violations**: Publishing others' private information without permission
- **Professional Misconduct**: Plagiarism, scientific misconduct, or misrepresentation
- **Disruption**: Sustained disruption of discussions or community activities
- **Intimidation**: Threats, aggressive behavior, or intimidation
- **Unethical Conduct**: Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews)
- Documentation and wiki contributions
- Community forums and chat channels
- Social media interactions related to the project
- Events or conferences where members represent the project
- Private communications regarding project matters

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Reporting Violations

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by:

1. **GitHub Issues**: For public, non-sensitive matters
2. **Email**: Contact project maintainers directly (see repository contacts)
3. **Private Message**: Use GitHub's private messaging for sensitive reports

### What to Include

When reporting, please include:

- Your contact information
- Names (usernames) of individuals involved
- Description of the incident
- Context and timeline
- Any supporting evidence (links, screenshots, etc.)
- Whether the incident is ongoing

### Confidentiality

All reports will be handled with discretion. We will protect the identity of reporters to the extent possible while addressing the issue effectively.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Scientific Integrity

As a scientific and medical modeling project, we maintain additional standards:

### Research Ethics

- **Reproducibility**: Code and methods must be reproducible and transparent
- **Validation**: Claims must be supported by appropriate validation and references
- **Literature**: Properly cite all sources and prior work
- **Data Integrity**: Never fabricate, falsify, or misrepresent data or results
- **Conflicts of Interest**: Disclose any potential conflicts of interest

### Clinical Responsibility

- **Patient Safety**: Always prioritize patient safety in clinical applications
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Respect medical device regulations and standards
- **Scope Limitations**: Clearly document model limitations and appropriate use cases
- **Medical Advice**: This software is for research/education; not medical advice

### Collaborative Science

- **Open Science**: Support open science principles and reproducible research
- **Credit**: Give appropriate credit for ideas, code, and contributions
- **Peer Review**: Participate constructively in peer review processes
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Share knowledge and help others learn

---

## Appeals

If you believe you have been wrongly accused or unfairly sanctioned, you may appeal by:

1. Contacting project maintainers with your appeal
2. Providing additional context or information
3. Requesting reconsideration of the decision

Appeals will be reviewed by maintainers not involved in the original decision when possible.
