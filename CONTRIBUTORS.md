## Special Thanks

### Scientific Foundations

This project builds on decades of pioneering research in cardiovascular physiology and computational modeling. We gratefully acknowledge:

#### Foundational Models

- **Balthasar van der Pol & J. van der Mark (1928)**: Cardiac relaxation oscillator theory
  - Original work on nonlinear oscillations in heart rhythms
  - Foundation for cardiac dynamics modeling

- **Richard FitzHugh (1961)**: Simplified neural excitability model
  - Two-dimensional reduction of Hodgkin-Huxley equations
  - Enabled tractable neural dynamics analysis

- **Alan Lloyd Hodgkin & Andrew Huxley (1952)**: Ion channel dynamics
  - Nobel Prize-winning work on action potential generation
  - Basis for modern neural and cardiac electrophysiology

#### Baroreflex and Autonomic Control

- **Mark W. Chapleau & François M. Abboud (2001)**: Baroreflex adaptation mechanisms
  - Mechanistic understanding of baroreceptor function
  - Foundation for autonomic control modeling

- **Dwain L. Eckberg (1997)**: Sympathetic restraint of baroreflex
  - Interactions between sympathetic and parasympathetic systems
  - Clinical baroreflex sensitivity testing

#### Heart Rate Variability

- **Task Force of the European Society of Cardiology (1996)**: HRV standards
  - Standardized measurement and interpretation of HRV
  - Clinical application guidelines
  - Reference ranges and methodologies

- **Marek Malik et al.**: HRV methodology and clinical applications
  - Advanced signal processing techniques
  - Clinical risk stratification

- **Maria Teresa La Rovere et al. (1998)**: Baroreflex sensitivity prognostic value
  - Clinical validation of baroreflex measurements
  - Risk stratification after myocardial infarction

#### Clinical Hemodynamics

- **Otto Frank (1895) & Ernest Starling (1918)**: Frank-Starling mechanism
  - Fundamental cardiac physiology principle
  - Basis for pressure-volume loop analysis

- **Kiichi Sagawa et al.**: Ventricular elastance theory
  - End-systolic pressure-volume relationship
  - Ventricular-arterial coupling

#### Drug Cardiotoxicity

- **Comprehensive in vitro Proarrhythmia Assay (CiPA) Initiative**: Modern cardiotoxicity assessment
  - Multi-parameter ion channel screening
  - Mechanistic risk assessment
  - Regulatory science advancement

### Software and Tools

- **Python Community**: NumPy, Matplotlib, Jupyter, pytest, and countless other tools
- **D Language Community**: ldc2 compiler, dub package manager
- **APL Community**: Dyalog APL and reference implementations
- **Git and GitHub**: Version control and collaboration infrastructure

### Educational Resources

- **PhysioNet**: Open-access physiological signal databases
- **OpenCOR**: Cellular and physiological modeling platform
- **BioModels**: Repository of computational models
- **PubMed and PubMed Central**: Open-access scientific literature

## How to Become a Contributor

We welcome new contributors! Here's how to get involved:

1. **Read the Documentation**: Start with [README.md](README.md) and [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md)
2. **Pick an Area**: Code, documentation, testing, community support
3. **Find an Issue**: Check [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/STLNFTART/Multi-Heart-Model/issues)
4. **Make a Contribution**: Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) guidelines
5. **Submit a Pull Request**: Your contribution will be reviewed and merged

### Good First Issues

Look for issues tagged with:
- `good first issue` - Great for new contributors
- `documentation` - Help improve docs
- `help wanted` - Community input needed
- `testing` - Add test coverage
