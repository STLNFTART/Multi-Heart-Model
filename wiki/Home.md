## 🎯 What is Multi-Heart-Model?

The Multi-Heart-Model is a comprehensive, validated cardiovascular modeling platform featuring:

- **Heart-Brain Coupling**: Bidirectional neural-cardiac interaction models
- **Autonomic Regulation**: Mechanistic baroreflex and ANS models
- **Clinical Education**: 7 interactive Jupyter notebooks
- **Drug Screening**: Multi-organ toxicity assessment
- **Research Tools**: HRV analysis, hemodynamic simulation, parameter validation

## 🚀 Key Features
